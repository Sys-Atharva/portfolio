# DESIGN.md — "Digital Studio" Design DNA

## Palette (Fresh & Corporate)
- Base: deep slate-dark `#0B0F19` (not absolute black)
- Primary accent: Digital Emerald `#10B981`
- Secondary accent: Nordic Teal `#06B6D4`
- Borders: cool-gray `border-slate-800`
- Typography color: high-contrast crisp white

## Typography
- Premium humanist sans-serif (Inter / Geist Sans)
- Headers: tight letter-spacing (`tracking-tight`)
- Body: highly readable, generous spacing

## Vibe
- Clean, editorial, trustworthy, exceptionally polished
- Subtle borders, spacious grid layouts, clean card shadows
- NO futuristic glows/scanlines. Dark mode only.
